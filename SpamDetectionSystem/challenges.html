6. Challenges Faced & How We Overcame Them

  • Challenge: Handling large and imbalanced email datasets.
    Solution: Applied stratified sampling and text normalization to balance spam/ham ratios.
  • Challenge: Model overfitting during training.
    Solution: Used cross-validation and regularization parameters to improve generalization.
  • Challenge: Limited time for live deployment.
    Solution: Built an efficient JavaScript simulation to demonstrate spam logic offline.
